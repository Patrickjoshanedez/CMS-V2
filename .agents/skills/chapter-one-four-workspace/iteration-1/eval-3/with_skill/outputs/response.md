## Revised findings and discussion
The supplied aggregate summary reports usability and satisfaction ratings among 77 respondents. Usability had a reported mean of 4.53 and standard deviation of 0.30; satisfaction had a reported mean of 4.48 and standard deviation of 0.34. Both means fall within the approved study band of 4.21–5.00, labeled Excellent. These statistics are reproduced from the supplied summary and were not independently recalculated because respondent-level data were unavailable.

The ratings describe favorable perceptions of the evaluated experience. Usability’s reported mean exceeds satisfaction’s by 0.05 points, but this numerical difference does not establish a meaningful or statistically significant difference. The reported standard deviations provide descriptive information about score dispersion; their interpretation depends on confirming the scoring unit and calculation procedure.

The study retains its adopted ISO/IEC 25010:2011 basis. Usability is associated with the product-quality model, while satisfaction concerns quality in use; the two reported scales should therefore not be presented as exhaustive coverage of product quality. Excellent is the study’s interpretation label, not an ISO passing threshold. These ratings do not demonstrate full ISO compliance or improvement relative to a previous process. The 200 ms benchmark is illustrative and is excluded from measured performance findings.

## Separate evidence and gap note
The supplied aggregate summary supports the four reported statistics and reported respondent count; the approved scale supports their Excellent interpretation. Confirm valid counts per scale, questionnaire items, weighting, missing-data handling, and whether SDs describe respondent composites. Raw data would permit verification and additional justified analyses. Comparative baseline evidence is required for improvement claims, and recorded workload, environment, repetitions, and timings are required for performance conclusions.
