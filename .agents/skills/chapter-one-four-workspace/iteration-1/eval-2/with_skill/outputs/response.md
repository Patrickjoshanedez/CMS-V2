## Revised methodology
The study uses a developmental-evaluative research design to structure system development and evaluation. Agile organizes iterative implementation; it is not itself the research design. Planned iterations will connect approved requirements to development tasks, review feedback, and recorded changes. Evaluation will examine selected requirements and instrument constructs using procedures specified before data collection. No supplied record establishes that testing has been completed.

The existing questionnaire adopts ISO/IEC 25010:2011. That edition will remain the stated basis, with its selected constructs and local adaptations documented. The instrument does not establish ISO 2023 certification. “Excellent” is a study interpretation category whose approved scoring boundaries must be documented; a favorable questionnaire category cannot establish that functional or technical tests passed.

The proposal targets 37 participants. A separate summary reports 77, but recruitment records are unavailable. These figures represent unresolved planning and reporting information, not a reconciled analyzed sample. Recruitment, eligibility, responding counts, exclusions, and valid-response totals must be established before reporting completed participation.

## Proposed reproducible test-record pattern
For each case, record: case ID; objective and requirement ID; application version; environment and user role; preconditions; precise input or fixture; numbered actions; expected observable outcome; acceptance rule; actual outcome; execution date and tester; evidence location; incident ID; and status. Expected outcomes belong to the plan. Actual outcome, execution evidence, and pass/fail status remain “not executed” until supported by a run. Record retests separately so failures remain traceable.
