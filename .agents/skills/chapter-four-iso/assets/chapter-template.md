# Chapter 4 Working Template

Follow institutional headings. The following pattern supports each objective; it does
not require five new subsections if the manuscript already uses an approved structure.

## Introduction
[Identify the objectives, evidence sources, and distinction between development outputs
and measured evaluation outcomes.]

## Findings for Each Objective
[State objective and evidence source. Introduce the artifact or result, present essential
values with units/n, explain the finding, and discuss its limits and relevant literature.]

### Table or Figure Discussion Pattern
[What the reader is seeing.] [Central finding.] [Meaning for the objective.]
[Comparison if supported.] [Material limitation or unresolved result.]

## Synthesis
[Summarize what the evidence establishes and what remains unmeasured. Supply a factual
basis for Chapter 5 without introducing new results.]

Checklist: every objective addressed; observed/expected separated; provisional numbers
excluded from empirical claims; correct n and units; ISO edition preserved; thresholds
attributed to the study; negative results retained; conclusions match evidence.

Expansion example: instead of "Table X shows excellent results", explain which construct
was rated, who rated it, the supplied mean and study-defined band, and what the rating
does and does not establish. Do not invent causes for high or low item ratings.
