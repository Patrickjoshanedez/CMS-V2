## 5.2.1 Testing Conclusion

The study established a plan for examining the platform’s functionality and reliability under the adopted ISO/IEC 25010:2011 framework. However, the available Chapter 4 evidence documents intended testing rather than executed outcomes. The functionality and reliability objective therefore remains unverified. The stated 200 ms response time is illustrative and cannot support a measured performance finding. A conclusion that the system passed ISO quality testing is not supported by the supplied evidence.

## 5.2.2 User Evaluation Conclusion

The supplied aggregate summary reports usability of 4.53 (SD 0.30) and satisfaction of 4.48 (SD 0.34), both classified as Excellent under study-defined bands. These reported results suggest favorable perceptions of the two separate constructs. Their interpretation remains provisional: the summary reports 77 respondents, whereas Chapter 3 planned 37, and recruitment records and raw responses are unavailable. The discrepancy prevents confirmation of the actual analyzed sample and its relationship to the approved method. The reported SDs also cannot be independently verified. These aggregate perceptions do not establish technical reliability or overall ISO conformity.

## 5.3 Recommendations

The research team should first reconcile the planned 37 respondents with the reported 77 using recruitment, eligibility, consent, and analysis records. Verification should produce one documented participant flow and a justified account of any methodological change. Neither figure should be selected solely because it supports a stronger conclusion.

The testing team should execute the functionality and reliability plan, retaining the build identifier, environment, expected and actual outcomes, and failure or recovery logs. Testing conclusions should be revised only after those records demonstrate results against predefined criteria. Replace the illustrative response-time figure with actual measurements if performance is evaluated.

The evaluation team should recover anonymized responses and the approved scoring rules, then verify construct membership, participant counts, means, and respondent-level SDs. Preserve usability and satisfaction as separate constructs. Until these checks are complete, retain qualified conclusions and identify the chapter as incomplete with respect to empirical verification.
