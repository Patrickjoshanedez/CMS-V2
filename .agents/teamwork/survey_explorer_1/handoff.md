## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Premise 1 (R3 Compliance):** Academic capstone workflows require official physical paper deliverables (Approval Sheets, Endorsement Forms, Defense Minutes, Rubric Scoresheets, Completion Certificates, Originality Reports) for defense binders, panel deliberation, and CHED/accreditation compliance.
2. **Premise 2 (Backend Availability vs Frontend Disconnect):** The server has already engineered automated multi-signatory digital approval sheets (`projectArchive.service.js:_generateFinalApprovalSheet`) and exposed them at `/api/projects/:id/approval-sheet`, and exported `getApprovalSheetUrl` in `authService.js`.
3. **Deduction 1:** Because no component in `client/src` imports or invokes `getApprovalSheetUrl`, students and faculty have zero access to the official Capstone 3 Final Approval Sheet. Exposing this deliverable in `CertificatePage.jsx` and `ActionDoneMatrixTab.jsx` closes a critical institutional gap with minimal frontend effort.
4. **Premise 3 (Printing Failure Modes):** When a component has `onClick={() => window.print()}` without accompanying `@media print` CSS (as in `PlagiarismReportPage.jsx` and `SophisticatedDocumentViewer.jsx`), or when inside a Radix Dialog (`LiveDefenseMinutesModal.jsx`), the browser executes default print routines that retain background shades, print interactive controls, and clip content at the bounds of `overflow-auto` and `fixed` containers.
5. **Deduction 2:** Injecting global print unrolling rules in `index.css` for `.docx-outer-container` and `[role="dialog"]`, while styling `PlagiarismReportPage` with `print:hidden` on action ribbons, will immediately solve manuscript and report print clipping system-wide.
6. **Premise 4 (R1 Sizing & Ergonomics):** `DashboardLayout.jsx` establishes a canonical `max-w-7xl` frame. Components that hardcode smaller max-widths (`max-w-5xl` in `CanonicalDocumentViewer.jsx`) or oversized dialogs (`max-w-6xl` in `VersionHistory.jsx`) disrupt shell continuity. Concurrently, touch targets below 44px on 390px viewports violate WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA.
7. **Deduction 3:** Applying responsive touch floors (`min-h-[44px] sm:min-h-0 sm:h-8`), removing `max-w-5xl` overrides, demoting the duplicate `<h1>` in `CanonicalDocumentViewer.jsx`, and wrapping dense forms in semantic `<fieldset>` elements will bring the frontend into full compliance with `cms-ui-consistency-library` and `cognitive-ergonomics-ux`.
